Critical care research and practice · Efficacy of Eye Movement Desensitization and Reprocessing (EMDR) on Anxiety Severity Among Physicians and Nurses Working in Intensive Care Units.

#2 🧠 高實用性

被動或潛意識再暴露於眼動介入中足以引發恐懼調節，挑戰 EMDR 核心假設

Behaviour research and therapy · Passive or subliminal re-exposure is sufficient for fear modulation in eye-movement intervention.

無需主動創傷記憶檢索，僅以被動或潛意識刺激結合眼動，即可產生持續的恐懼減緩效應（特別是減少入侵性思維），支持 EMDR 之治療機制可能經由下意識情緒調節路徑運作。

P健康參與者經過恐懼條件訓練（實驗1 N=56；實驗2 N=83）
I被動或潛意識再暴露於條件刺激 + 雙側眼動
C無眼動或無再暴露的控制條件
O被動再暴露降低即時皮膚電反應；潛意識再暴露減少一週內入侵性思維頻率與情緒強度；無意識時情緒困擾反而較低

#4 ♿ 高實用性

併發 ASD 特質之 PTSD 成人接受密集創傷聚焦治療（含 EMDR）之療效：高 ASD 特質亦獲顯著獲益

European journal of psychotraumatology · The impact of intensive trauma-focused treatment on adults with PTSD and ASD traits: a pre-post intervention study.

#5 ⚕️ 中實用性

EMDR 對 psychosis 伴創傷者之 PTSD 症狀與陽性症狀之六個月追蹤效應：系統性回顧與 meta 分析

· The effectiveness and tolerability of trauma-focused interventions for trauma symptoms in individuals who have psychosis: A systematic review and meta-analysis.

OEMDR vs TAU：PTSD 症狀顯著降低（post: g=0.84；6-month: g=0.63）；陽性 psychotic 症狀短期改善（g=0.21，p=0.05）但無六個月維持

· A pilot study of the feasibility and preliminary outcomes of sequential TF-CBT and EMDR online group therapy for adult women with histories of childhood sexual abuse.
